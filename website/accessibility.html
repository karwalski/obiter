Accessibility Statement

Obiter is built to be used by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Last reviewed: 30 June 2026

Our Commitment

Obiter automates the precise, repetitive work of AGLC4 citation — a load that is itself a barrier under tremor, fatigue, low vision, or cognitive strain. Accessibility is therefore central to what Obiter is for, not an afterthought. We treat WCAG 2.2 and ATAG 2.0 as the authority for accessibility in the same way the Australian Guide to Legal Citation is the authority for citation.

Conformance Target

  • WCAG 2.2 Level AA across the website, the Word task pane, and the documents Obiter produces.
  • Selected Level AAA criteria adopted deliberately for our audience: enhanced target size (2.5.5), reduced-motion support (2.3.3), and enhanced focus appearance (2.4.13) on primary controls.
  • ATAG 2.0 Part A (the tool is operable by authors with disabilities) and Part B (the footnotes, bibliography, and styles Obiter writes are themselves accessible).

What We Have Done

  • Full keyboard operability, including a keyboard-operable citation search (combobox), with a visible, high-contrast focus indicator.
  • A Comfort mode that enlarges targets and text, widens spacing, and disables motion in a single switch.
  • Respect for your system settings: reduced motion, Windows Contrast Themes / forced colours, and light/dark themes.
  • A persistent, re-readable status area instead of disappearing messages, so actions never fail silently.
  • Native Word footnotes and real heading styles, so the documents Obiter writes read correctly in screen readers and Word's navigation pane.

Known Limitations

Accessibility is ongoing work. Some areas are still being improved, including screen-reader polish across every advanced workflow and captioning of demonstration media. If you hit a barrier we have not yet fixed, please tell us — your report helps us prioritise.

Giving Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of Obiter, and all of our support is text-based and asynchronous — there is no requirement to make a phone call or use voice.

  • Email: accessibility@obiter.com.au
  • Contact form: obiter.com.au/contact
  • Open an issue on GitHub

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within five business days.
