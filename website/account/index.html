Your account

You manage your Obiter account from inside Word.

Obiter accounts sync your settings and let the relay use your stored API key across devices. Signing in, storing a key, and deleting your account all happen in the Obiter task pane in Word, under Settings › Account. The account itself never stores your citations or documents.
